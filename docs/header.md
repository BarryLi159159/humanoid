# Humanoid

A curated, opinionated map of **humanoid robot learning** papers from **2025-01 to now**, grouped by topic. Each paper gets a one-line **novelty** and a one-line **limitation**, and each topic starts with an overall summary: what the field is working on right now and what is still unsolved.

**Scope.** Papers whose first public version (usually arXiv v1) appeared in or after January 2025, either accepted at a venue or still a preprint. Humanoid-specific work is prioritized; a few legged/mobile-manipulation papers are included when they directly shape humanoid methods.

**How to read the tables.**

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Venue | Filled only when confirmed by a public source during curation. `arXiv` = preprint *or* venue not yet verified (many are accepted — PRs welcome). |
| `*` after a venue | Curator believes it is accepted there, but it was not re-verified for this list. |
| † after a paper | Title/abstract-level entry. Novelty is paraphrased from the title/abstract; the limitation is the curator's structural assessment (what this *class* of method cannot do), not a finding from the paper. |
| Limitation (all rows) | The curator's assessment unless it directly restates a result from the paper. Not the authors' own claims. |

**Coverage.** Not exhaustive. Seeded from [awesome-humanoid-robot-learning](https://github.com/YanjieZe/awesome-humanoid-robot-learning), conference lists (CoRL 2025, RSS 2026), and arXiv search through late September 2026. Missing papers are almost certainly an omission, not a judgment.
